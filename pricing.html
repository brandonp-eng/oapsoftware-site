Common questions

Pricing & access FAQ

Why isn't pricing listed publicly?

Because the right plan depends on your team, your sources, and how you deploy. As a founder-led, early-access product, we'd rather set a fair price in a short conversation than anchor you to a number that doesn't fit.

Do I bring my own API keys?

Yes. The platform is built around direct, transparent integrations, so you can use your own keys for the sources and models you trust, and keep control of those costs.

Can I run it in my own environment?

The platform is designed to run with minimal cloud dependency. Air-gapped and self-hosted deployments are handled under the Enterprise & on-prem plan.
