Security & Trust

Trust is a property of the architecture.

An intelligence platform is only as credible as its ability to show its work and protect what it touches. OAP is engineered for auditability, data minimization, and lawful, responsible use from the ground up.

Auditability

The system can always answer “why do you believe this?”

Every consequential judgment is recorded to an inspectable, append-only audit trail, from how the requirement was understood to the chain that connects each finding to its evidence and its confidence. Nothing important happens off the record.

Relationships are backed by the evidence that supports them. This audit layer is what makes the ethos enforceable: after truth, and what we can prove, and able to show the proof.

What the audit trail gives you
  • A recorded basis for every key judgment in the case.
  • A traceable chain from each claim back to its evidence.
  • An append-only record, the trail can be reviewed, not rewritten.
  • Confidence you can explain and defend to a reviewer.
How we protect data

Built for control over the data you handle

Open-source intelligence only

The platform works with open, lawfully accessible sources. It is a discipline for reasoning over public information, not a tool for intrusion or unauthorized access.

Air-gap-friendly deployment

Designed to run with minimal cloud dependency and direct, transparent integrations, so sensitive case data can stay inside your own environment.

Strict context separation

Internal case administration, scope, and authorization notes are walled off from the analytic process by design. Your internal notes can never leak into a search query or surface in the final product.

Analyst-controlled redaction

Analysts can black out irrelevant or sensitive content in the material they provide. The platform respects those redactions and never attempts to recover blacked-out content.

Provenance preserved

Nothing is silently discarded. When the platform resolves a reference to what it really means, it records where that came from, so the trail behind every conclusion stays intact.

Held to a standard

Output is held to a defined standard before it ships, from a clearly understood requirement through to a truthful, evidence-backed report. Quality is verified, not assumed.

Responsible use

A tool for analysts who take the work seriously

OAP is intended for lawful intelligence, due-diligence, research, and security workflows where each user holds the appropriate authorization for the case they run. The platform supports that responsibility, it does not replace it.

  • Authorization is recorded per case and kept separate from collection.
  • Outputs are labeled as fact, judgment, or gap, never blurred together.
  • Disconfirming evidence is presented alongside supporting evidence, no slant.

“The difference between research and intelligence: research finds information; intelligence finds information for a customer, analyzes it, and forms an actionable product that answers exactly what was asked, and can prove it.”
